# RIDELIN (`رایدلین`) — Official Persian Logo Suite (`APPROVED-FA/`)

This directory contains the canonical, approved Persian logo suite for **RIDELIN (`رایدلین`)** using **Shabnam Bold (`شبنم Bold`)** typography and the **Option L3 (`360px`)** short horizon speed blade line.

---

## 1. Approved Architecture & Specifications

- **Persian Typography:** **Shabnam Bold (`شبنم Bold`)** (SIL Open Font License 1.1 by Saber Rastikerdar).
- **Horizon Speed Line (`یِ خط`):** **Option L3 (360px short speed line)** positioned at `y="52"` to provide clean breathing space below the bottom dots of the Persian letter `ی` (`Ye`).
- **Colorway Palette:**
  - **Primary Black Version:** Matte Black (`#0D0D10`) / Charcoal background with Primary Burgundy (`#8A2747`) horizon blade and Ivory (`#FAF7F2`) typography.
  - **Primary Ivory Version:** Ivory (`#FAF7F2`) background with Primary Burgundy (`#8A2747`) horizon blade and Charcoal (`#17171A`) typography.
