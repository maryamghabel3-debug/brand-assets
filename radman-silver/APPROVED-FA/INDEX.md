## 2. Transparent Background Rules (CRITICAL)

To prevent visual degradation or low contrast, strict background pairing applies to all transparent PNGs:

1. **`radman-fa-primary-ivory-transparent-1600.png` & `radman-fa-tagline-ivory-transparent-1600.png`:**
   - **Artwork Color:** Ivory (`#FAF7F2`)
   - **USE ONLY ON:** Dark, Matte Black (`#0B0B0E`), deep charcoal, or deep luxury photo backgrounds.
   - **NEVER USE ON:** White, Ivory, light gray, or cream backgrounds.

2. **`radman-fa-primary-black-transparent-1600.png` & `radman-fa-tagline-black-transparent-1600.png`:**
   - **Artwork Color:** Matte Black (`#0B0B0E`)
   - **USE ONLY ON:** Light, Ivory (`#FAF7F2`), white, or light cream backgrounds.
   - **NEVER USE ON:** Black, charcoal, dark gray, or dark photography backgrounds.
