## 2. Evaluation Analysis & Recommended Top Two Fonts

### ⭐ Recommended Option 1: Gandom (`گندم`) — *Traditional & Classic Luxury*
- **Why it Excels:** Gandom features high-contrast stroke modulation that echoes the French Didot serif curves of English `RADMAN`. Its terminals create a timeless, regal harmony with the 8-pointed Royal Shamsa ornament.
- **Best For:** Classic jewelry branding, certificate of authenticity, high-end packaging.

### ⭐ Recommended Option 2: Estedad Bold (`استعداد`) — *Modern Chic Luxury*
- **Why it Excels:** Estedad Bold offers sophisticated geometric luxury proportions reminiscent of international luxury maisons (such as Cartier or Bulgari). It provides exceptional legibility in digital website headers and mobile viewports.
- **Best For:** E-commerce website headers, digital campaigns, modern minimalist packaging.

---

## 3. Options Omitted from Recommendation

- **Option C (Noto Kufi Bold):** While geometric and clean, its Kufic architectural blockiness feels slightly harsh against the delicate curves of the Shamsa and English serif wordmark.
- **Option D (Noto Naskh Bold):** Its book-text calligraphic style feels editorial rather than a proprietary luxury jewelry brand wordmark.

---

## 4. Final Recommendation Summary

We officially recommend **Option A (Gandom)** and **Option B (Estedad Bold)** for the business owner's final selection. Both options perform exceptionally across minimal header lockups and extended packaging lockups. No final winner is declared until explicit owner selection.
